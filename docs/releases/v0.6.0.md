## Highlights
- **Pin, bookmark, swipe.** Pin messages to the top of a chat, bookmark them (Gallery → "Starred"), swipe a bubble right to reply and left for message info (delivered/read per person). Drafts now follow you across devices.
- **More from the AI assistant.** "What did I miss?", summaries of voice messages, and detection of appointments, tasks and shopping items with one tap to Nextcloud Calendar, Tasks or Bring!. Reply suggestions can be switched off.
- **Notes to self, bulk messages and statistics** with a year-in-review card.
- **Deleted and edited messages are kept**: see what was deleted and the edit history.
- **GIFs.** Search and send GIFs (GIPHY or KLIPY, API key required). Attachments, emoji and GIFs live behind one plus button in the composer.
- **Better previews.** Emails are shown as clean text with "View original email"; files open in-app (PDF, Word, Excel, images, video, audio); link previews in the Links tab.
- **Videos** show a still frame with duration in the bubble and play full screen with sound on tap.
- **New look.** Ocean blue is the new default colour and logo (all colours selectable), haptic feedback, per-chat chat design, bubble styles, animated reactions and emoji messages, typing shown as three dots.

## Also since 0.5.1
- Chat list shows sent/delivered/read ticks and photo/video icons before the preview; unread badges are always red and clear instantly
- Images appear right after app start (image cache, permanent server thumbnails), loading spinners on media
- More reliable push: per device, also for late messages, none for backfilled history, notifications cleared when you open the app
- Email: archive and delete buttons in the chat header, delete without confirmation (optional)
- Refined send animation, fixed drafts that stayed after sending, no white area below the composer on iPhone
- App start shows the last state instantly and keeps you signed in
