## Highlights
- **English interface.** Chorus now speaks English and German. "Automatic" picks German on German devices and English everywhere else; switch under Settings → Language. Summaries, AI replies, AI search, live translation and push notifications follow the same language.
- **ChatGPT as a second AI provider.** Choose Claude or ChatGPT in Settings → AI assistant. Any OpenAI-compatible service (Ollama, LM Studio, OpenRouter …) works too, even locally without a key.
- **Tidier settings.** Messengers have their own page with a status card ("All connected · synced 3 min ago") and a Sync button. The AI page is split into text, voice and privacy, with saved keys shown as a status.
- **Appearance.** Tap a colour dot to open the palette or pick any custom colour, one uniform bubble colour is now selectable, and there are **chat backgrounds** (8 presets or your own photo, per device).

## Also since 0.4.1
- Email: archive and delete, also for whole senders
- Photo mosaics for several photos, the full-screen viewer swipes through albums, double-tap zoom on iPhone
- Save to Immich and Nextcloud, attach from Immich and Nextcloud, in-app file preview
- Failed messages: see why, resend or delete
- iMessage-style send animation (can be turned off)
- Swipe left to delete, right to archive; filter bar can be hidden
- Revoke AI access per chat; WhatsApp terms-of-service warning before linking

## Upgrade
```bash
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```
With automatic updates set up, nothing to do. The app language follows your device; German users keep German.
